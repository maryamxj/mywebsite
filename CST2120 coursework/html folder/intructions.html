Pizza Mystery game

Make sure to read each statement carefully, look at the clues and figure out whose lying and stole the last slice

How the game works

  1. Click on each character to read their statments
  2. Look at the clues and compare what each character says
  3. Choose the person you that stole the pizza
  • Be careful a wrong answer costs you a life
  • You have 3 lives in total
